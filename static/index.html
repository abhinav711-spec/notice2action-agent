How Notice2Action checks a notice

      
01 · Secure ingestion
Validate the PDF signature and size, extract page-indexed text, and scan for suspicious embedded instructions.

      
02 · Structured extraction
Identify notice details, requirements, and deadlines using the configured language model or the deterministic offline fallback.

      
03 · Evidence audit
Verify extracted quotes against the source page. Unverified claims are identified rather than presented as grounded facts.

      
04 · Profile comparison
Compare supported eligibility criteria with the applicant information you supplied and flag potential conflicts.

      
05 · Action planning
Prepare prioritized actions, a calendar file, and a draft clarification email for you to review.
